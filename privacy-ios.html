Privacy Policy iOS

Floppy Bird Disc — iPhone & iPad  ·  Effective May 6, 2026  ·  New Look Entertainment d.o.o.

This policy applies to Floppy Bird Disc as distributed on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad (com.void.floppydisc, iOS/iPadOS). We respect your privacy and keep data collection to the absolute minimum required to run the app.

Data We Collect

Floppy Bird Disc does not use advertising, sell data, or track you across apps and websites. The only data the app may handle is listed below.

Data type Why Where it goes
Game Center scores & identity To power the global leaderboard feature (optional — requires Game Center sign-in) Apple's Game Center servers only. We never see or store this data ourselves.
High scores & settings To remember your best scores and preferences Stored locally on your device only. Never transmitted.

Advertising & Tracking

Floppy Bird Disc contains no advertising and does not track you. We do not use the AppTrackingTransparency framework because no tracking takes place — there is nothing to request permission for. No advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or fingerprinting libraries are present in the app binary.

App Store Connect Privacy Nutrition Label

The iOS app's declared data uses are:

  • Game Center — leaderboard scores (linked to your Apple ID by Apple, not by us)
  • No advertising data, no tracking, no data sold to third parties

Game Center

Global leaderboards are powered by Apple Game Center. Your participation is optional — you can play without signing in to Game Center and no data will be collected at all. When you do sign in, Apple's own Privacy Policy governs how Game Center handles your information.

Local Storage

High scores, settings, and installed themes are saved in the app's sandboxed local storage. This data remains on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. It is not backed up to iCloud or transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party SDKs

No third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs are embedded in the app. The only external framework used at runtime is Apple's own Game Center (GameKit).

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the only external data flow is through Apple's own Game Center — which has its own age-gating — parents can safely allow children to play without a Game Center account and no data will be collected.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data on our own servers, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. To remove Game Center scores, use the Game Center settings on your device or contact Apple support.

Changes to This Policy

If we change our data practices, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use of the app after any change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
