Privacy Policy tvOS

Floppy — Apple TV  ·  Effective May 6, 2026  ·  New Look Entertainment d.o.o.

This policy applies to Floppy as distributed on the Apple TV App Store (com.void.floppydisc, tvOS). We take your privacy seriously and have designed the tvOS version of this app to collect no personal data whatsoever.

Data We Collect

None. The tvOS version of Floppy does not collect, store, transmit, or share any data about you or your device. Specifically:

  • No account registration or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs are included.
  • No tracking, profiling, or cross-app data collection takes place.
  • No personal identifiers, device identifiers, or usage data leave your Apple TV.

App Store Connect Privacy Nutrition Label

Our App Store Connect declaration for the tvOS app is "No data collected". We do not track users, serve ads, or share any information with third parties.

Local Storage

All game data (high scores, settings, installed themes) is stored exclusively in the app's sandboxed local storage on your Apple TV. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

The tvOS version of Floppy does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or frameworks that collect data.

Children

Because we collect no data at all, Floppy is safe for users of any age. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this page and revise the effective date above. Continued use of the app after any change constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
